## Versioning Policy

- The version in `App.tsx` is located at the bottom in the footer (e.g. `v1.0.1`).
- The version in `package.json` is located in the `"version"` field (e.g., `"1.0.1"`).
- Always increment the patch version (or minor/major if applicable) before completing your changes.
